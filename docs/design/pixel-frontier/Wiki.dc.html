The game's rules and numbers, the same for everyone. Every figure here comes from the game's current balance, so you can plan with it. It never tells anything about the world's current state.

The map is a rectangle whose opposite sides join: walk off the east side and you come back in from the west. It has no edge and no centre.

Terrain & walking#terrain

Terrain sets how fast residents move across a tile and how much food they burn there, compared with Plains. Agility softens the speed penalty, Endurance the food penalty.

TerrainSpeedFoodNotes
Plains100%100%Farmland
Forest~70%100%Wood
Hills~70%130%Stone, some ore
Mountain~35%200%Ore-rich; no carriages except on a road
Lakeimpassable—Small inland water
Seaimpassable—A few large bodies of water
Road~130%100%Built by Players on top of passable land

A diagonal step is about 1.41 times as far as a straight one, and can't squeeze between two corners.

Game API#api
CallCostAnswersWhat it does
store(place)[COST]—Hands over to the built-in Store order.
fetch(goods, place)[COST]—Hands over to the built-in Fetch order.
wait()[COST]—Not run again until the current order ends or the resident is struck.
end(reason)[COST]—Ends the script and notes the reason in the realm log.
