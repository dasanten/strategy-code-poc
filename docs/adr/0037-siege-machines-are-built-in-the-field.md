# Siege machines are built in the field

Status: decided (2026-11-15, with Daniel). Rules in `docs/context/military.md` under **Siege machine**; built in
Epic 110.

## Decisions

- **A machine, not a building, not a carriage.** It is laid out and built where it is needed, from
  goods carried there, so a siege needs a supply line like an army. Once finished its crew pushes
  it slowly; no horse pulls it, so it doesn't fit carriages and stables.
- **Two kinds for now.** A ram, whose crew is safe from above under its roof and has to be met by
  defenders coming out; a catapult, which throws Stone at walls from beyond a bow's reach and hits
  buildings only.
- **Players only, for now.** NPC cities and the Trade Organization don't build them; their wars go
  on as before.
