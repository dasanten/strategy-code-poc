# Trade Organization warehouses never drop below a floor

Status: decided (2026-10-18, with Daniel). Revised the same day: originally "refill without
limit", changed to a floor so the Trade Organization takes part in the real economy.

The game's core rule is that everything is physical: goods and money only change place by being
carried. The Trade Organization's stock is produced and bought physically like everyone else's:
it runs workplaces on every kind of resource and buys from others. We make one controlled
exception: each of its warehouses tops itself up to a **floor** (a minimum stock of every good,
including coins) when it runs low. Everything that leaves a warehouse is physical again and can be
robbed.

## Why

The Trade Organization has to be a dependable anchor for the economy: always able to buy and
sell, never bankrupt, always able to pay its army and answer theft with force. A fully simulated
supply chain could be starved out, and the world would lose its market of last resort. Unlimited
refill, on the other hand, would cut it off from supply and demand. With a floor, it is involved
in every resource, its prices react to real scarcity (a war that cuts off iron makes iron
expensive everywhere), and it still can't be emptied.

## Consequences

- Its stats, residents and army follow normal rules. The floor applies to goods, never to people.
- Its warehouses are a headquarters (the largest walled city on the map) and trading posts across
  the regions. Goods move between them by caravan.
- Its prices depend on its stock: low stock means high prices.
- No other actor gets a floor. Any further exception needs its own ADR.
