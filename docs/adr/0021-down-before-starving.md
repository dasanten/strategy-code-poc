# Starving puts a resident Down before it kills

Status: decided (2026-11-02, with Daniel).

A resident or horse without food died after about 5 game days (2 real hours). A Player who was
away for an evening came back to dead residents and horses with nothing they could have done
once they noticed.

## Decisions

- **Hunger puts a resident or horse Down instead of killing it.** Down, they lie unconscious on
  their tile for a while (a balance number, about one real day), do nothing and lose no more
  health. Not revived in time, they die of hunger as before.
- **Anyone can revive them** by giving Food: a grown resident of any realm on or next to the tile
  Feeds a resident; a horse wakes once it eats Food in reach. They wake at low health, idle.
- **Down is not safe:** anyone on the tile can take what they carry (theft from another realm),
  and an attack kills them.
- **Only hunger.** Wounds kill as before; a fight has no Down phase.

## Alternatives

- **Any health at zero puts them Down**, combat included. Makes fights less final but needs a
  separate "finish off" rule and changes every combat balance number.
- **Longer starving, no Down phase.** Same grace, but nothing for others to do, and the resident
  keeps acting while starving.

## Why

The game runs while Players sleep; a real day is the window in which a Player can notice and act.
Letting other realms revive (or rob) a Down resident makes it something that happens in the
world rather than a timer only the owner sees.
