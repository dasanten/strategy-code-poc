# Demands are instant words with terms

Status: decided (2026-11-04, with Daniel).

Players, and the Trade Organization, want to tell someone "give the city back or we attack". The
game had no way to say that, and no way to hand over a city short of being conquered.

## Decisions

- **It arrives at once,** like a mercenary offer. This is a deliberate exception to news travelling
  by people; a witness's news of a crime still travels physically.
- **Terms and a note.** A demand holds structured terms (a named city, goods or Coins), a short
  written note and a deadline. Sending is limited, and the note's length is capped.
- **The threat is only words.** Nothing happens by itself when a demand is ignored. The game forbids
  nothing and enforces nothing; the sender acts or doesn't.
- **Accepting carries out the terms:** a city joins the sender's realm at once, goods and Coins are
  set aside for the sender to fetch or sent by a Transfer.
- **NPC cities yield to force in sight.** They accept when the respect they see from the sender far
  outweighs their soldiers, otherwise they decline and their standing drops. The Trade Organization
  always declines.

## Alternatives

- **A messenger walks it there.** Fits "everything is physical", and a messenger can be stopped, but
  a demand across the map would take game weeks to answer.
- **Paid through the Trade Org.** Limits spam, but stops working when the HQ falls, and is odd for
  its own demands.
- **Only free text.** NPC cities couldn't understand it, and a promise to hand over a city would
  need a separate way to do it.
- **NPC cities never yield.** Few Players are online, so demands would rarely be used.
