# What the Trade Organization can't take back stays a debt

Status: decided (2026-11-04, with Daniel). Changes the Trade Org reaction, which said it never
captures a city.

When its army came for what was stolen and the storages were empty, it went home and that was the
end of it. A thief only had to keep their storages empty. Daniel: when it can't recover, it should
compensate in other ways, like taking over the city or destroying production sites.

## Decisions

- **What it couldn't take stays owed,** as a debt of that Organization, payable at any warehouse.
- **It goes up steps while the debt is owed:** a demand with a deadline, then its army tears down
  workplaces worth the debt, then after a set number of trips it holds the town hall and the city
  becomes a new trading post. Paying in full stops it at any step.
- **A captured post it has heard of is a debt too,** and giving it back settles it.
- **Its army can reach far cities and feed itself:** a supply wagon, and buying Food on the way.
- **Admin directives stay as they are,** with no debt.

## Why

It closes the loophole, and the first two steps give the culprit a clear way out, so one careless
theft doesn't cost a city out of nowhere.

## Consequences

- The Trade Organization can now hold more cities than it started with, so its number of posts can
  grow. It still never founds one, and its army keeps being limited by spare soldiers.
- "The Trade Organization never captures" goes from `CONTEXT.md`, and the invariant that says so
  has to change.

## Alternatives

- **Demand, then wreck only.** Keeps it a market rather than a conqueror, but a thief with no
  workplaces left owes nothing and gets away.
- **Straight to capture.** Simple but very harsh for one unnoticed theft.
