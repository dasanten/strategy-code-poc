# Horses are sold in person, by NPC cities

Status: decided (2026-11-05, with Daniel). Changes Horse, which said the Trade Organization sells them.

Daniel: NPC cities should have horses, and an option to sell them to or buy them from Players. Today
they start with none and no horse can be bought or sold anywhere, though `CONTEXT.md` said the Trade
Organization sells them.

## Decisions

- **NPC cities have horses:** two to start with, by migration for those already standing, then bred
  up to a number by size. They feed their stables, which nothing did before.
- **Trade is in person at the city's stable,** Coins in hand, and the horse stays where it stands.
- **The price follows the horse:** stats, age, and how many the city has against how many it wants,
  moved by standing.
- **The Trade Organization doesn't sell horses for now,** and the rule says so.

## Why

- Trading anywhere in sight would be easier to use, but a horse would change hands away from anyone.
- A fixed price would make breeding good horses pointless.
- A Trade Org market would need stables and horses of its own there, and a last resort isn't needed
  while every NPC city keeps some.

## Consequences

- NPC-run stables eat Food, so that goes in the reserve.
- A daughter city has no horses until a Player sells it some, since NPC cities don't trade horses
  with each other.
- A stolen horse sells like any other, so horse thieves have a market. Witnesses and standing still
  apply.
