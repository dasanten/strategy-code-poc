# Organizations and territory: NPC stances

- **Temperament** (Epic 98): what an NPC city is like, fixed when it comes into being: **merchant**,
  **militarist** or **settler**; a daughter mostly takes its mother's. It tilts which stance the
  city picks and how it weighs its jobs.
- **Stance** (Epic 98): what an NPC city is after for now, picked again each game day from its
  needs, threats and neighbours, tilted by its temperament:
  - **Grow:** houses, farms and workplaces as its needs say (NPC city).
  - **Fortify:** more soldiers than by its size, walls, stone and a training ground first, gates
    shut sooner, bigger escorts.
  - **Trade:** more traders going further, and a smaller surplus cap, so more goes to market.
  - **Expand:** counts as full sooner, scouts more, founds more often, and goes for nodes it lacks
    (Taking a node).
  - **Retaliate:** turned on one Organization that hurt it (Retaliating).
  Whoever has traded with a city or sees it reads its temperament on its card; whoever sees it
  reads its stance as a mood line ("arming", "busy trading"). Scripts see the same. Its stance and
  temperament weigh into every job's score, as do the danger places it remembers by the way to a
  workplace (decided overnight, Daniel to confirm: distance and crew weigh nothing yet), so the
  order of its jobs can change: a dangerous far mine can lose to a safe near one. Food short
  still comes first. How it picks (decided overnight 2026-11-17, Daniel to confirm): at its first
  planning pass of each game day, each stance has a need: Fortify by the threat it feels from
  the one Organization it fears most (not all together: armed neighbours it merely sees would
  add up, T98.9), Trade with a Food surplus and a Trade Org warehouse in its traders' reach,
  Expand with a Food surplus and nothing short; its temperament's own stance (merchant Trade,
  militarist Fortify, settler Expand) weighs more, and the greatest full need wins, else Grow.
  Under Fortify danger places weigh more on its jobs. Not yet: Fortify putting walls, stone and a
  training ground first, and Trade's traders going further (a later ticket).
- **Threat** (Epic 98): how dangerous an NPC city thinks an Organization is, apart from how it
  regards it (Standing): from that Organization's armed residents it saw near itself or its people,
  by their gear, and from its people that Organization killed or robbed (traders, scouts, escorts).
  It fades over game days. A strong friendly neighbour can be a threat. The city also remembers
  **danger places**, where it lost people, to anyone, bandits too: escorts grow and its people's
  ways keep clear of them while it remembers. A killing or robbery raises threat once the city
  learns of it, as it does Standing; a danger place is marked where its person was killed outside
  its City area, at once (decided overnight 2026-11-17, Daniel to confirm).
- **Acting first** (Epic 98): an NPC city doesn't only answer. On its own it invites those it holds
  in high standing into an alliance against a common threat; hires mercenaries with its war chest
  when threatened, as a Player can (Trade Org army, Hiring from an NPC city); sends soldiers after
  residents it saw commit a crime against it, within its reach, to take back what was taken; and
  sends demands (NPC demand). Decided overnight 2026-11-17, Daniel to confirm: it seeks allies and
  hires once its threat from one Organization reaches a balance number; it invites the realms in
  no alliance it holds in high standing (never the one it fears) and the NPC cities that hold it
  in high standing and feel threatened by the same Organization, into an alliance it leads,
  founding one (named after itself) if it has none; an NPC city that founded an alliance leads
  it, and the role passes to a realm first when it leaves. It hires from the Trade Org army only,
  only soldiers standing at the HQ and only while its town hall lies within its trader's reach of
  the HQ (#1414, decided overnight, Daniel to confirm),
  as many as a balance number for a balance number of days, with the whole price in its
  storages, paying each week from them like a transfer; its mercenaries stand at its town hall
  and fight attackers in its City area. They set out with the Food for the walk there and back
  from a storage of their own city, as a trader does, and in its City area they eat from its
  storage as at home, up to the Food for the walk home, so they go home fed when the contract
  ends (#1414: they starved without; decided overnight, Daniel to confirm). Its
  soldiers guarding a site likewise set out with the Food for the way, and one down to what the
  walk home eats goes home and leaves the escort, which it fills again with others.
  - **Hunting a thief:** for a theft it knows of (seen by one of its residents, or the news
    brought home), a few soldiers (a balance number per thief) go after the thief while they stay
    within its reach of its home (a balance number, its set reach); only soldiers beyond its hall
    and gate posts go, those on its wall first. They strike the thief down, which is no crime
    wherever it happens, take what was stolen from the pile where the thief fell (as much of it
    as lies there) and carry it into the city's storage, then go back to their posts. A thief who
    gets out of reach is let go. Decided overnight, Daniel to confirm: the soldiers know where
    the thief is while in reach, and only goods are taken back, not a stolen carriage or horse.
- **NPC demand:** an NPC city in Retaliate, or a militarist one, that far outweighs an Organization
  sends it an ordinary Demand for goods or Coins with a deadline, never to the Trade Organization.
  Paid, its threat and standing towards the payer settle; refused or lapsed, it retaliates.
  Decided overnight 2026-11-17, Daniel to confirm: a militarist city sends one to a realm it knows
  of (holds a threat from, or a standing either way) whose armed residents, wherever they are, its
  own side far outweighs, at its first planning pass of a game day; Players' realms only for now;
  it asks for Coins (a balance number), and the next to the same realm only after a set time. It
  fetches nothing set aside, so it is paid only by a Transfer to it, which the Player can send
  without seeing the city. Paid means accepted: its threat from the payer is gone and its standing
  toward them back to neutral at least; refused or lapsed, its standing falls to hostile. Retaliate
  (T98.8) sends them too.
- **Retaliating:** an NPC city in Retaliate turns its soldiers on one Organization: escorts and
  hunting thieves first, demands, and rarely an attack on one of its cities to take it (Capturing a
  city), only with very high threat and very low standing and an army that far outweighs that
  city's defenders. It ends when its threat from them has faded, a demand is paid, or its losses
  leave it the weaker; then it turns to Fortify, and an attack under way is called off.
  Decided overnight 2026-11-17, Daniel to confirm: it turns on a realm (only a realm for now) whose
  threat reaches a balance number while it knows of a recent crime of theirs against it, picked
  at its first planning pass of a game day like any stance, and checks each planning pass whether
  it is over (faded below a balance number, a paid demand clearing it, or its strength below
  theirs). It sends that realm demands as a militarist city does. A march takes its soldiers beyond
  its hall and gate posts to the town hall of the realm's city nearest it; held to the end, that
  city leaves the realm with its residents and goes on as an NPC city of its own (the NPC city
  takes no city into itself). Whoever sees it reads "retaliating against" and the realm's name.
- **Taking a node** (Epic 98): an NPC city in Expand that is short of a good it has no node for
  goes for the nearest node of it that it knows. One nobody works it builds on, sending its crew
  with an escort. One another Organization holds only a militarist city takes, and only when it far
  outweighs that node's guards: its soldiers drive the workers out and its own move in (Holding a
  workplace). That is an attack like any other, and a crime against the holder. Never against a
  realm in its shelter. Decided overnight 2026-11-17, Daniel to confirm: the escort goes with the
  crew building on a node out of its City area, a few of its soldiers beyond its hall and gate
  posts standing at the site until it is built. A node is taken only when no free one of the good
  is known, and only a workplace in no city that a realm holds (not an NPC city or the Trade
  Organization). Its holders are that realm's residents within a few tiles of it, working or not;
  its guards' strength is that of the armed ones among them, and the city sends soldiers until they
  far outweigh it. They strike everyone of the holder there until none is left up; then the
  workplace is the city's. With none of its soldiers left up it calls it off.
