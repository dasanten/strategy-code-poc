# Crime and reputation

- **Crime:** theft, attack, killing or tearing down buildings against another organization. In a
  city's own City area, striking someone occupying its town hall, or a thief of its realm that the
  realm knows of or one of its residents saw, is no crime; for an NPC city, the same for the city.
  Someone occupying is someone armed standing on the hall who could hold it, also once a defender
  stepping onto the hall has broken the occupation; one who has walked off it no longer is.
  Striking anyone else there still is.
- **Witness:** a resident who sees a crime (within sight range), or a victim who survives it. A
  crime only becomes known when a witness physically brings the news home. No witness, no
  consequences.
- **Stealing a carriage or horse:** a grown resident on the tile of another organization's
  carriage or free horse can take it, by driving the carriage off, by mounting the horse, or by
  hitching the horse when they drive off in a carriage with none hitched. It joins the thief's
  realm at once, a carriage with its load and hitched horse, and from then on is an ordinary
  carriage or horse of that realm. It can't be taken while someone drives it, rides it or is
  aboard, nor while an armed resident of its owner is on its tile and none of the thief's realm
  is (guarded like an outpost storage). A stable doesn't protect a horse. A Down horse can't be
  ridden or hitched, so it is Fed first. Each taking is one theft (Crime, Witness), worth the
  carriage, the horse and the load together; taking back what was stolen from you is theft like
  any other. The victim's realm log says at once that it is gone and where it was last; who took
  it becomes known only through a witness. Taking one that belongs to nobody is no crime.
  **A vessel** is stolen the same way by a grown resident beside it rowing (or sailing) it off,
  with its load; "on its tile" reads "beside it" for its guards.
- **Standing:** how one organization regards another. Known crimes lower it; trade and kept
  contracts raise it; it slowly drifts back to neutral. Standing is per organization, so allies
  don't suffer for each other's crimes. A Player sees their standing toward others but it does
  nothing on its own; the game acts on it only for NPC cities and the Trade Organization.
- **NPC city reaction:** at low standing an NPC city won't trade with you or hire out to you
  (Trading with an NPC city, Hiring from an NPC city) and keeps its gates shut to your residents; the lower it is, the more it asks to be bought, and at
  very low standing it won't sell at all.
- **Persuading a city:** an NPC city that holds you in high standing and has seen enough respect
  from you for a set time (your fighters near it outweighing its own side without a break, Respect)
  asks to join your realm. You accept or decline.
- **Respect:** how dangerous a place looks to others, judged from the armed residents and walls
  they can see. Bandits avoid targets with high respect. **What an NPC city weighs** when a realm
  demands it or it thinks of joining one (ADR 0033): on the realm's side only its armed residents
  standing in the city's area or in sight of it, seen by a resident of the city who is at home,
  each by their strength as combat counts it (weapon, armour and shield, Combat skill); the realm's
  walls don't count, nor what the city's people see far from home, nor residents the city lent the
  realm. On its own side its soldiers, weighed the same way, and its finished walls and gates. The
  city remembers since when a realm has outweighed it without a break; the first planning pass it
  doesn't, that time starts again from nothing.
- **Trade Org reaction:** below one standing threshold it refuses to trade; below a lower one it
  sends its army to take back the value and punish: to the culprit's city nearest the crime, to
  carry home twice what it lost (coins first), fighting whoever resists. It leaves once it has what
  it came for; what it couldn't take becomes a debt.
- **Trade Org debt:** what the Trade Organization came for and couldn't take stays owed by that
  Organization, however long it takes, from when its army heads home or is lost, whatever the reason.
  It says so in the realm log, and a resident on any warehouse's tile can pay it in Coins at any time,
  in part or in full, from their hands or a carriage there, even if the Trade Organization otherwise
  refuses them. It rises by what later crimes cost: a crime it comes to know of while it is owed adds
  what it cost, once, and sends no army of its own. An Organization that is gone takes its debt with
  it. While it is owed the Trade Organization goes up these steps, one after the other (ADR 0025), and
  paying it in full stops it at any step:
  1. **It demands:** a demand (Demand) for the debt, with a deadline, once for each debt. It always
     asks for what is owed now, and paying in full at a warehouse ends it. Declined or lapsed, the
     next step starts at once. Accepted, what is handed over comes off the debt as a caravan brings it
     in, from the warehouse nearest: set aside at a storage in a city, for no fee, or sent by a Transfer,
     for a fee as always. What that leaves owed starts the next step at the deadline.
  2. **It wrecks:** past the deadline, its next army goes to that city and tears down workplaces of
     it, each counting for its worth against the debt, until the debt is covered or none are left.
     Those who resist are fought. A workplace is that city's if it is on its books or one of its
     residents works it, also while away carrying; not an idle one nobody of it works, nor one on
     another Organization's books or that someone of another Organization works. Once its army has
     found one at the city it stays on its list, even if the workers leave or die, unless another
     Organization takes it over.
  3. **It takes the city:** with a debt still owed after a set number of trips, its army occupies the
     town hall (Holding the town hall). The city joins the Trade Organization as a new trading
     post: its storages become warehouses with what is in them, but for the Food, which goes into a
     new storage near its town hall that its people eat from, its people stay as the post's, and the
     debt is settled. A trip counts once its army has reached the city: one lost or turned back on
     the road doesn't, and a city behind shut gates it can't reach never comes to this step. Its
     soldiers fight anyone armed standing on the hall, as those block an occupation, and one stays on
     the hall when the others go for Food. It doesn't give up while its occupation is running; broken
     and past its time, it goes home and the next army comes. With no room for a new storage, the
     storage holding the most Food stays one and takes all the Food, and a city with no other has no
     warehouse. A city with no storage at all gets an empty storage and an empty warehouse. Workplaces
     its residents worked that aren't on its books stay the post's own, and its gates open as it joins.
     Its army travels with a **supply wagon**, when the HQ has a free adult to drive it: a new Trade Org
     carriage and horse, loaded at the HQ with Food for the whole trip as far as that leaves room for what
     it takes. It follows the army (Supply line) and its soldiers refill from it, go to meet it when they
     run out with it out of reach, and put on it what they take when it is near. It is robbed like a
     caravan's: taking from it or stealing it is a crime and its soldiers go after those who do, and its
     crew is lost like one (Caravan crew lost): a soldier takes over from a dead driver, unless they are
     the last, and with no crew left the HQ sends a new driver on foot, who packs for the walk. Stolen,
     or with its horse dead, it is gone for the army, which goes on with what it carries. It comes home
     with them, or by itself once they are lost, and its whole load goes into the warehouses; the carriage
     and horse go back into the HQ's stock. And short of Food on the way its army
     buys some from a post, NPC city or Player's offer it passes. Each soldier packs Coins at the HQ for
     this and hands in what is left back home. A soldier is short when the rest of the trip takes more
     than they carry and their share of the wagon's Food; with Coins left, they go to such a place near
     them and buy by the same trade anyone uses, cut down the same way, and carry on. An NPC city that
     refuses the Trade Organization sells them nothing. They don't buy from the Organization they are
     punishing, from one the Trade Organization refuses to trade with, or at an offer asking too much.
     Coins and Food they brought or bought are their own: none of it counts as what the army took. With
     no seller near they go on hungry. A captured post the Trade Organization
     has heard of (Lost post found out) is a debt too, for its worth, added to what is owed: it demands
     the city back. Returning it takes the post's worth off the debt; anything else owed stays, and gets
     its own demand for Coins. A demand for Coins already open stays open and rises by the worth, and
     refusing the post then leaves the next step to that demand's answer. With only the post's demand
     out, no demand for Coins goes too. A city they no longer hold can't be given back, so news of it
     makes an ordinary debt with a demand for Coins. Given back, its warehouse turns back with all it
     holds; if they tore that down, another storage takes its place, keeping the one its people eat from,
     and with none spare a new empty warehouse stands on the old spot. The post gets a caravan with a new
     crew, carriage and horse. Armed residents left on its hall start a new occupation as anyone's do
     (Occupying a town hall), and taking it again is a new crime. Admin directives go on as before, with
     no debt.
- **Trade Org army at an NPC gate:** an NPC city that doesn't refuse the Trade Organization keeps its
  gate open while only the Trade Organization's army is near, so its soldiers can walk in and buy
  like anyone. A city that refuses it shuts them out as it does anyone it refuses, and other armed
  outsiders near still shut the gate.
- **Demand:** a Player, the Trade Organization or an NPC city (NPC demand) sends a demand to another Organization: **terms**
  (hand over a named city; hand over goods or Coins), a short written note, and a deadline. It
  arrives at once, like a mercenary offer, wherever the other side is, and sending them is limited.
  A Player can send one to any realm they know of: one they see residents of, an ally, one whose city
  they have seen (Last seen), one with a standing toward them or they toward it, or their side in a
  contract, demand or Direct offer. Both sides see it in their Diplomacy tab's Demands until it is answered, and a notice
  tells the one it is sent to. They accept, decline or let it lapse; the sender hears that at once,
  and it lapses too when either side's Leader dies. The note is plain text.
  - **The threat is only words.** Ignoring a demand does nothing by itself. Whatever was threatened
    is the sender's to carry out, or not. The game forbids nothing.
  - **Accepting** carries the terms out: a named city joins the sender's realm at once, as a captured
    one does (What joins with a city), and goods and Coins are set aside at a storage of the one who
    accepts, for the sender alone to fetch (a Direct offer in person for each good, wanting nothing
    back, and fetching it is no trade: no standing changes), or sent by a Transfer from a city of
    theirs to a city of the sender's whose storage they see, one load at a time, the fee theirs. The
    one accepting picks which, and where. If the storage is short it says so before they accept, and
    what it holds is what goes; with none of it there they can't accept that way. A city no longer
    theirs can't be handed over. Handing over is the Player's own choice, also for their last city,
    which goes as losing it to capture does: no crime and no standing lost.
  - **An NPC city** accepts once the sender's fighters near it have far outweighed its own side
    (Respect) without a break for a set time, so an army that stays at the gate can take a city
    without a fight. With none of the sender's fighters near it declines at once; otherwise it waits,
    **wavering**, and declines when the deadline comes first. Declining drops its standing toward the
    sender. Both sides see in the Diplomacy tab's Demands that it wavers and how long is left, or that the
    time has started again. It carries out goods the
    same way: set aside at its storage, for the sender alone to fetch; with none of them there it
    declines. **The Trade Organization** always declines, and being asked costs standing. A Player
    knows of an NPC city as of a realm, and everyone knows of the Trade Organization. Both look at a
    demand at their planning pass, within a game hour (ADR 0016), where the Trade Organization
    answers and an NPC city answers or goes on wavering; a city that has joined a realm by then
    lets the demand lapse.
